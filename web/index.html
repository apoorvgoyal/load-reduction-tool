Help & glossary

Peak load
The most heating or cooling the building needs in any one hour on an extreme design day. The plant is sized for it.
Design day
An extreme day from ASHRAE's climate data for the weather station: hot (cooling) or cold (heating). "0.4%" means only 0.4% of the hours in a typical year are hotter; "99.6%" means only 0.4% are colder.
Ton (of cooling)
12,000 Btu per hour, or 3.517 kW. The usual US unit for cooling plant size.
MBH
1,000 Btu per hour, or 0.293 kW. The usual US unit for heating plant size.
Instant estimate
A model trained on hundreds of EnergyPlus runs for this building type and location. It answers instantly; its tested accuracy is shown with every result.
Full simulation
EnergyPlus, the US Department of Energy's building energy simulation engine, run on your exact inputs in your browser.
